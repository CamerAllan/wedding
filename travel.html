Getting thereCómo llegar

Rowallan Castle is approximately a 30-minute drive from Glasgow city centre.Rowallan Castle está a unos 30 minutos en coche del centro de Glasgow.

Please park at the Glasshouse restaurant, a 2-minute walk from the castle entrance.Por favor, aparcad en el restaurante Glasshouse, a 2 minutos a pie de la entrada del castillo.

If you would like to book a taxi, we recommend that you book in advance, especially for the journey home. Please note that Uber does not operate in East Ayrshire.Si queréis reservar un taxi, os recomendamos hacerlo con antelación, sobre todo para la vuelta. Tened en cuenta que Uber no funciona en East Ayrshire.
